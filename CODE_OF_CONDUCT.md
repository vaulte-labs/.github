# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in Vaulte's issues, pull requests and discussions is treated with respect,
whoever they are and however much they know.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Don't publish anyone's private information, including what they shared with you in confidence.

The maintainers may edit or remove anything that breaks these rules, and block anyone who keeps
breaking them. To report something, choose **Report content** from the comment's **…** menu.
